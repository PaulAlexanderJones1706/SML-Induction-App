Sydney Markets

Site Safety
Induction

For tenants, staff, drivers, buyers, workers and visitors (except public shoppers at Paddy's or the Flower Market).

Everyone entering Sydney Markets must complete this induction before going on site. It explains how to stay safe around the markets and takes about 5 minutes.

We collect your name, contact details, photo and induction record so Security can verify your induction on site. See the Sydney Markets privacy policy.

Before you start

Safety Briefing

You’re about to view 11 safety slides and 1 short video.

Please take your time to read each slide carefully and watch the video before continuing.

If anything is unclear, ask someone to help explain it before you proceed.
